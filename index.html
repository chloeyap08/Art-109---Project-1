Banned Films

Films Kanopy

Throughout the history of cinema, films have been banned, censored, edited, or removed from public view for many different reasons. Some challenged political systems while others dealt with religion, sexuality, violence, race, war, or social issues that governments and institutions considered dangerous or inappropriate. Sometimes film was banned because of a single scene but some was because of the ideas behind the entire film were seen as a threat. This project is an archive of those films and the stories surrounding them. Rather than looking at banned films only as controversial entertainment, this archive looks at censorship as a reflection of the society that created it.

Banned Magazines

Magazines

In some cases, magazines were banned because they criticized political leaders or governments. Others were targeted for publishing material connected to protest movements, counterculture, feminism, LGBTQ+ communities, underground art, or other ideas that challenged social norms. Some were restricted because of nudity, explicit imagery, or language. Others were banned simply because authorities feared the influence they could have on readers. The goal of Banned Magazines is not to celebrate every publication or agree with everything printed inside them. Some magazines contain material that may be offensive, disturbing, outdated, or harmful. Instead, the archive documents why these publications were restricted and allows visitors to examine the historical context surrounding those decisions.

Banned Websites

Websites

Websites can be banned for all kinds of reasons. Some talk about politics or criticize governments. Others share news, art, social movements, religious ideas, or content that certain groups find controversial. In some cases, a website might be available normally in one country while being completely blocked in another. This archive is not here to say that every banned website was good or that every website should stay online forever. Some websites can contain harmful or illegal material. The point is more about looking at why websites get blocked and what those decisions say about who controls information online.
